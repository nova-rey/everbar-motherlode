# Attribution

Motherlode does not claim ownership of third-party datasets. Build-specific attribution is generated at `reports/attribution.md` from the included source records.

For sources accepted for automated acquisition on 2026-08-21, the permanent
source and citation record is in [THIRD_PARTY_DATASETS.md](THIRD_PARTY_DATASETS.md).
The generated report includes only datasets that actually reach `DONE` in that
build; pending, failed, or review-required sources are never claimed as used.
